# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ManaKhata’s community to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes being respectful of different viewpoints, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
focusing on what is best for users and the community, and showing empathy.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing
others’ private information (including anyone’s financial details) without permission, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository owner’s GitHub profile or a private security
advisory. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
